❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan anytime? +
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan anytime. If you upgrade, you'll be charged the prorated difference. If you downgrade, credit will be applied to your next billing cycle.
What happens after my free trial ends? +
After your 14-day free trial, your hospital will need to choose a paid plan. You'll have 7 days to select before access is restricted. No charges will be made until you confirm your plan.
Do you offer discounts for annual billing? +
Yes! We offer 15% discount on annual subscriptions compared to monthly billing. Additionally, long-term contracts (2-3 years) qualify for additional discounts.
Is there a setup/onboarding fee? +
Basic setup is complimentary. However, custom integrations, data migration, and advanced onboarding services are available as paid add-ons (₹50,000-₹5,00,000 based on complexity).
How is data security handled? +
We maintain HIPAA, DISHA (India), and GDPR compliance with 256-bit encryption, regular security audits, and automated backups. Your data is stored in secure, redundant data centers.
Can I integrate with existing hospital systems? +
Absolutely! Professional and Enterprise plans include integration support. We can connect with most major HIS/EMR systems, lab management, pharmacy, and billing software.
What if I exceed my discharge limit? +
Overages are charged at the per-discharge rate (₹50-₹200 depending on plan). Alternatively, you can upgrade to a higher tier to increase your included limit.
Do you offer API access for custom integrations? +
API access is available with Enterprise plans at ₹0.50 per API call. We provide comprehensive documentation and sandbox environment for development.
